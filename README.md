## Open datasets and the reference sites built on them

I build small reference sites from official and openly licensed sources and publish the data behind each one. Every figure is read from the source named in the dataset's README and checked by program. Corrections: open an issue on the dataset's repository.

| Dataset | Site | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [Dog breed lifespan figures from four open-access studies](https://github.com/JasonTKG/dog-breed-data)<br>Median lifespan and life expectancy by dog breed, as published in four open-access (CC BY 4.0) studies, one row per study and breed. | [dog-breed-data.at01.io](https://dog-breed-data.at01.io/) | CC BY 4.0 |
| [The Psalms verse by verse in three public-domain English texts](https://github.com/JasonTKG/psalms-and-prayers)<br>All 150 psalms, verse by verse, in the King James Version, the American Standard Version and the World English Bible, with a per-psalm index. | [psalms-and-prayers.at01.io](https://psalms-and-prayers.at01.io/) | CC0 1.0 |
| [Dates of holidays observed in the United States, by year](https://github.com/JasonTKG/us-holiday-dates)<br>Computed dates of 35 federal, Christian, Jewish and other holidays observed in the United States, each confirmed against a published official table. | [holiday-dates.at01.io](https://holiday-dates.at01.io/) | CC BY 4.0 |
| [USDA PLANTS characteristics for 534 plants, one row per plant](https://github.com/JasonTKG/plant-data-sheets)<br>Conservation plant characteristics, native status and distribution counts for 534 plants from the USDA PLANTS Database, with the hardiness zone band of each plant's minimum temperature. | [plant-data-sheets.at01.io](https://plant-data-sheets.at01.io/) | CC0 1.0 |
| [Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023: sections, IPC correspondence and classification](https://github.com/JasonTKG/bns-sections)<br>The 358 sections of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 as enacted (Gazette of India), the government's IPC correspondence table and the BNSS First Schedule classification. | [bns.govsimple.org](https://bns.govsimple.org/) | CC0 1.0 |
| [Fiestas laborales locales, autonómicas y nacionales de España, 2026 y 2027](https://github.com/JasonTKG/calendario-festivos-locales)<br>Fiestas locales de 2027 de 1.167 municipios de Andalucía, Galicia y Canarias y fiestas nacionales y autonómicas de 2026 y 2027, leídas de los boletines oficiales. | [festivos.govsimple.org](https://festivos.govsimple.org/) | CC0 1.0 |
| [Philippine payroll rates and minimum wages from official issuances](https://github.com/JasonTKG/payroll-tables-ph)<br>SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contribution rules, withholding and income tax tables, pay multipliers and regional minimum wages, each tied to the official issuance it comes from. | [payroll-ph.govsimple.org](https://payroll-ph.govsimple.org/) | CC0 1.0 |
| [SNAP EBT monthly deposit-day rules by U.S. state](https://github.com/JasonTKG/ebt-schedule)<br>The day of the month SNAP benefits are loaded to EBT cards in 24 U.S. states, by case number, ID digits, name or birth month, each row cited to the state agency's page. | [ebt.govsimple.org](https://ebt.govsimple.org/) | CC0 1.0 |
| [India place, district and PIN code crosswalk](https://github.com/JasonTKG/india-place-district-pin-crosswalk)<br>27,794 places in India with state, district (LGD codes), PIN code, post office and coordinates. | [which-district-india.at01.io](https://which-district-india.at01.io/) | CC BY 4.0 |
